TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Common issues and how to resolve them

HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Throughout this guide, the V2MOne sensors mounted on the vehicle are referred to simply as “the sensors.” Issues are grouped by phase: setup, test drive, and reports. Find the symptom that matches what you're seeing in the Quick Reference Table below, jump to that section, and follow the steps in order — they go from the simplest fix to the most involved. If nothing here resolves the issue, see Getting further help at the end of this document.

Setup issues

Problems that occur before you start a test — while preparing the sensors, pairing them with the V2MOne app, or entering vehicle information.

Sensor won't turn on.

What it means. Pressing and holding the power button does not produce the single confirmation beep, and the sensor does not appear in the Devices list. Most often this means the sensor's battery is fully depleted.

What to do.

  1. Confirm you're using the correct power-on gesture: a single press and hold until you hear one beep.
  2. Place the sensor on the wireless charging platform and confirm the platform's LED turns blue.
  3. Leave the sensor charging for at least 15 minutes, then try the power button again.
  4. If the LED on the platform stays red instead of turning blue, see Sensor won't charge below.
  5. If the sensor still does not turn on after a full charge, contact V2M support.

Sensor won't charge (platform LED stays red).

What it means. The wireless charging platform shows a red LED instead of blue. The sensor is on the platform but is not aligned correctly with the charging coil, or the platform itself is not powered.

What to do.

  1. Confirm the power supply is plugged into a working outlet and connected to the platform via the USB-C cable.
  2. Reposition the sensor so it sits flat and centered on the platform.
  3. Wait a few seconds. The LED should turn blue once the sensor is correctly seated.
  4. If the LED stays red with both sensors and across multiple repositioning attempts, contact V2M support.

Sensor doesn't appear in the Devices list.

What it means. The sensor is turned on but the V2MOne app is not discovering it over Bluetooth.

What to do.

  1. Confirm Bluetooth is enabled on the phone and that the V2MOne app has Bluetooth permission in the phone's settings.
  2. Make sure the sensor is within a few feet of the phone.
  3. Pull down to refresh the Devices list in the app.
  4. Power-cycle the sensor. To turn it off: press the power button once, then press and hold it for about 5 seconds until you hear three beeps. Wait a few seconds, then turn it back on by pressing and holding the power button until you hear one beep.
  5. If the sensor still doesn't appear, contact V2M support.

Battery is below 40% before a test.

What it means. The V2MOne app reports a sensor's battery level below the 40% threshold required to start a reliable test.

What to do.

  1. Place both sensors on the charging platform and confirm the LEDs turn blue.
  2. Wait until both sensors reach at least 40% charge before starting the test. Charging time depends on the remaining charge and can take up to 3 hours to full battery. If the device is hot, allow extra time for it to cool down.
Tip

Keep the sensors on the charging platform between tests so they're ready to go when you need them.

VIN won't scan or vehicle info doesn't auto-populate.

What it means. The V2MOne app can't read the VIN from the source you pointed it at, or it read the VIN but the make, model, and year fields didn't fill in automatically from NHTSA data.

What to do.

  1. Confirm the V2MOne app has camera permission in the phone's settings.
  2. Make sure the VIN is in focus, well lit, and free of glare. If you're scanning the windshield plate, view it straight on rather than at an angle.
  3. Try scanning the VIN from a different source — door-jamb label, vehicle title, insurance card, or infotainment display.
  4. If scanning still fails, enter the 17-character VIN manually in the Testing tab.
  5. If make, model, and year don't auto-populate after a successful VIN entry, the vehicle may not be in the NHTSA database. Enter these fields manually and proceed with the test.

Sensors won’t connect to Wi-Fi.

What it means. You entered the network credentials and tapped Connect Sensors, but one or both sensor cards on the Devices screen still read “WiFi not connected” instead of showing a network name and IP address.

What to do.

  1. Re-open the Sensor WiFi screen and re-enter the network name and password exactly — both are case-sensitive, and one wrong character blocks the connection.
  2. Keep the phone or router close during setup so the sensors stay in range.
  3. Make sure the network is broadcasting on 2.4 GHz.
  4. If you’re using Personal Hotspot, confirm it's still on — iOS switches it off when no device has joined for a while.
  5. Tap Connect Sensors again and wait for both cards to show a green checkmark with an IP address.
  6. If a sensor still won't connect, power-cycle it and retry; if it still fails, contact V2M support.
Tip

A sensor is connected only when its card shows a network name and IP address. “WiFi not connected” means it isn't.

iPhone Personal Hotspot credentials are rejected.

What it means. The sensors won’t join your iPhone’s Personal Hotspot even though you entered a name and password.

What to do.

  1. In iOS, open Settings → Personal Hotspot and turn on Allow Others to Join.
  2. Check the network name: it’s your iPhone’s device name (Settings → General → About → Name), case-sensitive.
  3. Check the password against Settings → Personal Hotspot → Wi-Fi Password.
  4. Keep the Personal Hotspot screen open while the sensors join — iOS keeps the hotspot discoverable while that screen is visible.
  5. Re-enter the credentials on the Sensor WiFi screen and tap Connect Sensors.

Note: Don’t lock the phone or leave the Personal Hotspot screen until both sensors show a green checkmark — the hotspot may switch off before they connect.

External Wi-Fi shows a sign-in page (captive portal).

What it means. The sensors joined a public network (café, hotel, dealership guest Wi-Fi) but can’t reach the V2M server. These networks often require you to accept terms or sign in on a web page first, and the sensors can’t complete that step.

What to do.

  1. Use a network without a sign-in page — a private shop or home network, or your iPhone’s Personal Hotspot.
  2. Complete the sign-in page, then reconnect the sensors.
  3. Re-open the Sensor WiFi screen, select the new network, and tap Connect Sensors.
Tip

Personal Hotspot avoids captive-portal problems entirely and is the most reliable option away from a known network.

Tape strips won’t stick

What it means. The surface is too cold or not clean enough. The adhesive needs a degreased surface and at least 50 °F (10 °C) to bond.

What to do.

  1. Wipe both the sensor base and the mounting spot with isopropyl alcohol and let them dry.
  2. If the car just came in from the cold, let the underbody warm up above 50 °F (10 °C).
  3. Press hard over each strip for 10–15 seconds and wait 15–20 minutes before the drive.
  4. If the spot still won’t hold and the panel has openings or a reachable edge, mount with zip ties (Mode B) instead.
Tip

Leftover residue from a previous install rolls off with your thumb and IPA. Don’t scrape it.

Test drive issues

Problems that occur after the session has started — while you are driving the vehicle.
Note: The sensors record audio independently of the phone. Most test-drive issues do not cause the recording to be lost — but they may affect the route map, speed values, or the model's ability to grade the report.

Bluetooth dropped during the drive and didn't reconnect.

What it means. The phone lost its Bluetooth link to the sensors and did not reconnect by the end of the drive. The sensors continued recording on their own — no audio data is lost.

What to do.

  1. After the drive, bring the phone back near the sensors. The V2MOne app reconnects automatically as soon as the sensors are in range.
  2. Once reconnected, the recording uploads to the server and the report is generated as usual.
  3. If the V2MOne app does not reconnect within a few minutes of being in range, force-quit the app and reopen it.
  4. If the sensors still don't reconnect, contact V2M support.

Sensor came loose from the vehicle during the drive.

What it means. A cable tie (ICE) or adhesive pad (EV) failed during the drive, and a sensor moved from its mounted position. Recordings made after that point will not reflect the vehicle's normal acoustic signature and can lead to incorrect findings.

What to do.

  1. Pull over when it is safe to do so.
  2. End the current session in the V2MOne app and discard the report.
  3. Reinstall the sensor following the appropriate Sensor Installation Guide (ICE or EV).
  4. For ICE, use fresh cable ties. For EV, replace the adhesive pad if it has lost its tack.
  5. Start a new test.
Warning

Don't try to reattach the sensor while driving.

Phone shut down or restarted during the test.

What it means. The sensors continued recording — the audio side of the diagnostic is not lost. While the phone was off, the V2MOne app could not capture location data, so the missing segment will appear on the route map as a straight line, and the average and maximum speed values may be affected. The sensors will end the session automatically at the 10-minute mark.

What to do.

  1. Pull over when it is safe to do so.
  2. Power the phone back on and open the V2MOne app. The app will reconnect to the session in progress.
  3. Decide whether to keep the session or discard it. If your phone was off only briefly and the session is still within the 10-minute window, you can let it finish. If the session is largely lost, end it in the V2MOne app and start a new test.

Sensor rattles, buzzes, or came loose during the drive.

What it means. The mounting has lost tension or grip, so the sensor is vibrating against the vehicle. Recordings from this session may contain rattle that is not coming from the car.

What to do.

  1. Pull over when it is safe to do so.
  2. Tape mount: press each strip firmly for 10–15 seconds. If a strip has let go, clean the spot with IPA and apply a fresh strip.
  3. Zip-tie mount: check both ties. Replace any loose or stretched tie with a fresh one and tension it until the sensor can’t move by hand. Trim the tails.
  4. Start a new test from the Testing tab and let it run for at least 9 minutes for a guaranteed result.

Note. Re-mount the same way as the previous session on this vehicle — changing the mounting method changes how the sensors hear the car.

Session ended in under 5 minutes.

What it means. A session shorter than 5 minutes does not contain enough data for the V2M model to produce a reliable diagnostic. The report will fail.

What to do.

  1. Confirm the sensors are still installed and have at least 40% battery.
  2. Start a new test from the Testing tab and let it run for at least 9 minutes for a guaranteed result.

Session ended in the 5–9 minute window.

What it means. Sessions in this window may produce a result, but a confidence grade from the model is not guaranteed. If the report comes back without a grade, or if you want a higher-confidence diagnostic, run a new test.

What to do.

  1. Wait for the report to finish processing — it may still produce a usable result.
  2. If the report has no grade or you want a more reliable diagnostic, start a new test and let it run for at least 9 minutes.

Reports and results issues

Problems with the report itself — its contents, how it looks, or whether it appears at all.

Straight line on the route map.

What it means. Location capture was paused at some point during the drive — usually because the phone was off, the V2MOne app was not in the foreground (under the While Using the app permission), or the phone lost GPS signal. The missing segment is drawn as a straight line between where capture stopped and where it resumed.

What to do.

  1. To prevent this in future tests, set the V2MOne app location permission to Always Allow in the phone's settings.
  2. If you prefer While Using the app, keep the V2MOne app in the foreground for the entire test — don't switch apps or lock the screen.

Note: The straight line affects only how the route is drawn. The acoustic data captured by the sensors is unaffected, and the diagnostic itself is still valid.

Average or maximum speed in the report looks wrong.

What it means. Average and maximum speed are calculated from location data. If location capture was paused during the drive — same root cause as the straight-line issue above — the calculation is based on incomplete data and may not match what you actually drove.

What to do.

  1. To get accurate speed values in future tests, set the V2MOne app location permission to Always Allow.
  2. If you prefer While Using the App, keep the V2MOne app in the foreground for the entire test.

Report stuck in processing for more than 2 minutes.

What it means. The recording has been uploaded but the V2M model has not finished generating the report. This is almost always a connectivity issue — either the upload itself is slow on a weak cellular signal, or the model is waiting on data that hasn't fully arrived.

What to do.

  1. Confirm the sensors were connected to Wi-Fi during the test. On the Devices screen, each sensor should show a network name and IP address, not “WiFi not connected.” If the sensors had no Wi-Fi, the recording can’t upload and the report won’t progress — reconnect them (see Connect the sensors to Wi-Fi in the User Guide) and re-run the test.
  2. Move to an area with better cellular coverage or connect the phone to Wi-Fi.
  3. Pull down on the Reports tab to refresh the list.
  4. Wait a few more minutes. On a slow connection, processing can take longer than usual.
  5. If the report is still stuck after 15 minutes, contact V2M support and reference the test date and VIN.

Report has no grade.

What it means. The model received the recording but couldn't assign a confidence grade. The most common reasons are a session shorter than 9 minutes, sensors that came loose during the drive, or recording conditions that contaminated the audio (heavy rain, very rough roads).

What to do.

  1. Review the test conditions: was the session at least 9 minutes long, were the sensors firmly mounted, and was the road dry?
  2. Reinstall the sensors if needed and start a new test.
  3. If repeated tests under good conditions still produce no grade, contact V2M support.

No report appears after the test ends.

What it means. The session ended but no entry showed up at the top of the Reports tab. Either the recording never made it to the server, or the app hasn't refreshed yet.

What to do.

  1. Confirm the phone has an active cellular or Wi-Fi connection.
  2. Pull down on the Reports tab to refresh.
  3. Force-quit the V2MOne app and reopen it. The app will reconnect to the sensors and sync any pending recording.
  4. If no report appears within 15 minutes, contact V2M support and reference the approximate test time and VIN.

Getting further help

If you've worked through the steps above and the issue isn't resolved, V2M support is here to help: support@v2minc.com

When contacting support, please include:

  • The VIN of the vehicle you were testing.
  • The approximate date and time of the test.
  • A short description of what you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • Screenshots from the V2MOne app, if applicable.
