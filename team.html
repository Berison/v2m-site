Team

V2M builds AI that locates a vehicle fault by the sound it makes.

Founded in New York City in 2021 · Member of NVIDIA Inception · Patented

  • Petr Bakulov
    Founder and CEO

    Petr Bakulov is the founder and CEO of V2M and the inventor of its acoustic fault-localization method. V2M’s acoustic fault-localization method is patented in the United States. He holds a PhD in vehicle diagnostics, started out as a dealership mechanic, and has spent 15+ years building auto-service businesses. He is a race-winning driver (FSL class, 2020). Founded V2M in New York City in 2021.
